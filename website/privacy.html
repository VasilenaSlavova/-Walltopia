Privacy & Cookie Policy

What information the Walltopia Preliminary Loads application processes, why it is needed and what choices are available to you.

1. Who we are

Walltopia Preliminary Loads is operated by Walltopia AD, UIC 204143670, with registered address at 1B Bulgaria Blvd., 5570 Letnitsa, Bulgaria. Walltopia AD is the controller of personal information processed through this application.

2. Information we process

Depending on how you use the application, we process:

  • Account information: name, email address, password hash and account creation date. The password itself is not stored.
  • Project information: project name, calculator inputs, result summary, tags and additional project information.
  • Support information: questions submitted for a saved project, their status and submission date.
  • Technical information: the limited connection and browser information normally required to deliver and secure a web service.

3. Cookies and browser storage

Only technologies needed for login, security and interface continuity are currently used.

TechnologyPurposeDuration
wt_token cookieKeeps a registered user signed in. It is HTTP-only and is not available to page scripts.7 days, or until logout
Local storageStores a limited signed-in interface preview. It does not contain the password or authentication token.Until removed from the browser
Session storageTemporarily preserves interface state during the browser session.Until the browser session ends

The application does not currently use analytics, advertising or marketing cookies. If optional technologies are added, this policy and the consent controls will be updated before they are enabled where consent is required.

4. Accounts, saved projects and support

An account is required to save, reopen and manage projects. Each project is linked to its account owner and is not intended to be visible to other users.

Engineering Support inquiries can be submitted only through the dedicated Support page. They are stored with the selected project and may be delivered to the configured support email service.

5. Retention and deletion

Saved project information remains available until the account owner deletes the project. Deleting a project also deletes its associated questions. Authentication data expires after the period shown above; the account record itself remains until it is deleted following an approved request or applicable retention requirement.

6. External services

The interface requests web fonts from Google Fonts. When those fonts are loaded, the browser connects to Google infrastructure and sends ordinary connection information such as the device IP address. The application does not currently include third-party analytics or advertising services.

7. Your rights

Subject to applicable data-protection law, you may request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information, restriction of processing, data portability, or object to certain processing. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

8. Contact and policy changes

For privacy questions or requests, use Walltopia's official contact page. You can also review Walltopia's general privacy policy.

We may update this policy when the application, legal requirements or the services used to operate it change. The date at the top identifies the current version.
